Lab Director

Brittany S. Cassidy, Ph.D.

Associate Professor of Psychology · Office: 280 Eberhart Building

Dr. Cassidy is an Associate Professor of Psychology at UNC Greensboro, where she joined the faculty in 2018. She received her B.A. in Psychology from Boston University and her M.A. and Ph.D. in Psychology from Brandeis University, and completed postdoctoral training in the Department of Psychological and Brain Sciences at Indiana University. Her work has been funded by the National Science Foundation and the National Institute on Aging. She was named a Rising Star by the Association for Psychological Science (2018), was elected a Fellow of the Society of Experimental Social Psychology (2023), and received UNCG's Thomas Undergraduate Research Mentor Award (2023).

When she's not analyzing data or writing, Dr. Cassidy can be found distance running, organizing, watching Bravo (really!), or taking selfies with her human (Gloria) and canine (Lacey) daughters.
